# payments

Payment acknowledgements, one folder per year: `payments/2026/RCT-2026-001-nwb-deposit.md`.

- Record the payment in the register first, then issue the acknowledgement (`handbook/templates/payment-receipt.md`).
- Evidence is a bank or remittance **reference**. Never store bank account numbers, card numbers or bank statements here.
- Part payments, overpayments and tax withheld by the client are recorded in the register's Payments sheet.
