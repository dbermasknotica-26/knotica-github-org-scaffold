# 07-billing

**Client-facing billing documents only.** Everything here is visible to the client.

| What goes here | Example |
|---|---|
| Agreed billing schedule | `billing-schedule.md` (copy of `handbook/templates/billing-schedule.md`) |
| Invoices we issued (PDF) | `INV-2026-001-deposit.pdf` |
| Payment acknowledgements | `RCT-2026-001.pdf` |
| Credit notes | `CN-2026-001.pdf` |
| Monthly statements | `STMT-<CODE>-2026-10.pdf` |

Do **not** put internal margins, rate negotiations, collection notes or the finance register here. The source of truth is the internal `billing` repo.
Questions about an invoice: open a Service Request in this repo, or contact the billing contact in `00-engagement/`.
